# Get In Touch — references for the two social boxes

Reference images for the two small (still empty) dark boxes in the Get In
Touch section, which become the Instagram and Facebook links.

## Instagram box (`data-social="instagram"` in index.html)

- `instagram-profile-picture.png` — Irene's Instagram profile picture: the
  round "Irene Ellis Art" logo (woman's profile with a protea, tagline
  underneath) inside Instagram's orange-to-pink gradient ring.
- `instagram-handle.png` — the handle as Instagram shows it: **ireneellisart**
  (bold, white on dark).

## Facebook box (`data-social="facebook"` in index.html)

- `facebook-profile-picture.png` — Irene's Facebook profile picture: her
  business card (front and back, with the "Irene Ellis Art" script logo)
  inside Facebook's blue ring.
- `facebook-page-name.png` — the page name as Facebook shows it:
  **Irene Ellis ART** (bold, black on white).

These are screenshots kept as references for building the boxes. They are
small and low-resolution, so they are not meant to be used on the site as they
are. Nothing in the site loads this folder.
